ECF (Escrituração Contábil Fiscal) overview.
The ECF consolidates information used to calculate IRPJ and CSLL. It is filed
annually through the SPED system and replaced the former DIPJ.

Who must file.
In general, legal entities are required to file the ECF, including companies
taxed under Lucro Real, Lucro Presumido and Lucro Arbitrado. Companies under
Simples Nacional are generally exempt, with specific exceptions.

Relationship with the ECD.
The ECF recovers balances from the ECD (Escrituração Contábil Digital) of the
same period, so the ECD must be transmitted first and both must be consistent.

Common review points.
Check account mapping to the referential chart of accounts, LALUR/LACS records
(Part A and Part B), and consistency between ECD closing balances and ECF opening
balances.
